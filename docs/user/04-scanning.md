# Scanning a Document

## Scan both sides

1. Click **+ Neu** (*new*). A new, empty document appears in the list
   **Dokumente** (*documents*).
2. Put the card or passport on the glass, front side down.
3. Click **Vorn scannen** (*scan front*).
4. Wait. The status shows **Scan läuft …** (*scanning*), then
   **Verarbeitung …** (*processing*) and **MRZ lesen …** (*reading MRZ*).
   As soon as it says *processing*, the pictures are taken and you may move the
   card.
5. Turn the card over.
6. Click **Hinten scannen** (*scan back*).

When the status shows **Scan abgeschlossen** (*scan finished*), the document is
complete.

## Scan automatically

In **Einstellungen** (*settings*), switch on **Automatisch scannen**
(*scan automatically*). Now the scanner starts by itself when you put a
document on the glass.

- The first scan is the front, the next one the back, then the front of the
  next document, and so on.
- The program cannot see which side is up. It just takes turns.
- If you only want one side, click **+ Neu** before the next card. Otherwise
  the next card becomes the back of this one.
- Lift the card off completely between scans. A card that just lies there is
  not scanned again.

## Scan a side again

Select the document in the list and click **Vorn scannen** or
**Hinten scannen** again. The new picture replaces the old one.

## Tips for good pictures

- Keep the glass clean.
- Put the document flat and straight. Slightly crooked is fine.
- Do not cover the glass with your hand while scanning.
