# Scanning a Document

## Scan both sides

1. Click **+ New**. A new, empty document appears in the list
   **Documents**.
2. Put the card or passport on the glass, front side down.
3. Click **Scan front**.
4. Wait. The status shows **Scanning …**, then **Processing …** and
   **Reading MRZ …**. As soon as it says *Processing*, the pictures are taken and you may move the
   card.
5. Turn the card over.
6. Click **Scan back**.

When the status shows **Scan complete**, the document is complete.

## Scan automatically

In **Settings**, switch on **Scan automatically**. Now the scanner starts by itself when you put a
document on the glass.

- The first scan is the front, the next one the back, then the front of the
  next document, and so on.
- The program cannot see which side is up. It just takes turns.
- If you only want one side, click **+ New** before the next card. Otherwise
  the next card becomes the back of this one.
- Lift the card off completely between scans. A card that just lies there is
  not scanned again.

## Scan a side again

Select the document in the list and click **Scan front** or
**Scan back** again. The new picture replaces the old one.

## Tips for good pictures

- Keep the glass clean.
- Put the document flat and straight. Slightly crooked is fine.
- Do not cover the glass with your hand while scanning.
